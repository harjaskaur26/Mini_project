Paneer Butter Masala

Paneer Butter Masala is a creamy and tasty North Indian dish. It is made with soft paneer pieces in a rich tomato gravy. It goes well with roti, naan or rice.

Ingredients

  • 250 g paneer (cut in cubes)
  • 3 tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 onion
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp fresh cream
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the butter in a pan.
  2. Add the chopped onion and cook until it turns golden.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute.
  4. Add the tomatoes and cook until they become soft.
  5. Let the mixture cool, then blend it into a smooth paste.
  6. Pour the paste back in the pan. Add chilli powder, garam masala and salt.
  7. Add the paneer cubes and cook for 5 minutes.
  8. Add the cream, mix well, and switch off the flame.
  9. Serve hot with roti or rice.
