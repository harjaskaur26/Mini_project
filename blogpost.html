Learning to code can feel hard in the beginning. But if you take small steps every day, it becomes easy and even fun.

1. Practice Every Day

Even 30 minutes of practice each day is better than studying for many hours only once a week.

2. Build Small Projects

Make small things like a recipe page or a blog page. When you build something, you learn much more than when you only read.

"The best way to learn is to do."

3. Do Not Be Afraid of Mistakes

Every programmer makes mistakes. Each mistake teaches you something new, so keep trying and never give up.
